About this app

Vdesi Chat is a calm, distraction-free space for capturing thoughts the moment they happen. Quick-capture notes, voice-to-text jotting, and gentle daily prompts help you build a journaling habit without the pressure of a blank page. Organize entries into folders, tag them for later, and search everything instantly — even offline. A soft focus mode dims everything except the page you're writing on, and end-to-end encrypted backup means your private thoughts stay private. Whether it's a morning journal, meeting notes, or a fleeting idea on the bus, Vdesi Chat is built to get out of your way and let you write.
